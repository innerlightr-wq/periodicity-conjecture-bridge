3.3  What is NOT subsumed.
    MY 2.7(b) says nothing at density EXACTLY beta.  The foundation paper's own main
    theorem sits precisely there: its word 1c_beta has ones-density exactly beta, the one
    density excluded by both halves of the known dichotomy (MY 2.7(b) below beta, Lopez-
    Stoll Theorem 1 above it).  That result is NOT subsumed, and the foundation paper
    says so explicitly.  Nothing in this repository's Phases 2-9 sits at density beta:
      1c_beta ones-density                     = beta = 0.630929753571   <- NOT subsumed
      every CS Rote sequence                   = 0.500000000000   <- subsumed
    Also not subsumed: the abstract criterion itself (a mechanism, not a class theorem),
    and any future class placed at density beta.
