3b. rho = 0 is DEGENERATE and must be excluded from the target family.
    At rho = 0 the orbit point {0*alpha + 0} = 0 sits exactly ON the discontinuity of the
    partition, so for ell = q_k the pair ({m*alpha}, {m*alpha + q_k*alpha}) straddles it at
    m = 0: the period-ell agreement fails immediately and L - ell = 0.  The table above shows
    exactly that: every negative exact surplus occurs at rho = 0 with L - ell = 0.
    The target family is therefore stated with rho NOT in Z*alpha + Z.
