READING.  The constant is explicit but astronomical: the first M at which the *effective*
argument yields a positive surplus is around 10^17, at a witness length ell of about
10^(2*10^13) -- utterly beyond computation.  So the explicit route does NOT produce usable
numbers; it produces a WRITTEN-DOWN bound where previously there was only 'effectively
computable in principle'.  The five finite certificates (H >= 2^462 ... 2^15998) remain the
only numerically useful height floors, and they are Baker-free.

NO VALID REDUCTION TO INTEGER COEFFICIENTS EXISTS.  Clearing denominators in
  q * Lambda_m = (qm)(alpha log 3) + (p - q j_m) log 3 - q log 2      (rho = p/q)
gives integer coefficients, but the first logarithm is  alpha log 3 = log(3^alpha), and 3^alpha
is TRANSCENDENTAL by Gelfond-Schneider (alpha algebraic irrational).  Linear-forms-in-logarithms
theorems require algebraic arguments, so Laurent-Mignotte-Nesterenko and Baker-Wustholz do not
apply, with or without clearing denominators.
