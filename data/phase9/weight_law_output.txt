    class                                     off=-1   off=0    off=+1   exact?

    VERDICT ON THE WEIGHT LAW:
      (W-y) y-root, 0 < c_k < a_k          : exact in 952/952 cases
      (W-x) x-root with x(k) >= 1          : exact in 2032/2032 cases
      x-root with x(k) < 1                 : deviates in 27/27 cases  <-- the source of the D1 x-deviations
      intermediate                         : deviates in 85/2030 cases  <-- must read the actual weight

    So the parity argument is safe exactly on the BHZ-attained y-roots, and on x-roots
    whose exponent is at least 1.  For intermediate roots the parity must be computed
    from the word -- which is what scripts/phase9/phase9_even_branch.py does (it asserts
    w == sum(u[:ell]) on every root it uses).
