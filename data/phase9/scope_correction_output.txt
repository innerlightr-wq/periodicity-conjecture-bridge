    FINDINGS.
    (1) Thue-Morse's initial prefix powers top out near 1.6667 < 2, so it indeed has
        no initial square in this range -- the Phase 1 observation is correct.
    (2) But its density is exactly 1/2, giving g = 1.396241, and 11 of the
        lengths tested clear that threshold.  So the CRITERION is not vacuous on
        Thue-Morse for lack of squares; what fails is the GROWTH of the surplus,
        because r stays bounded below 2 while the penalty grows with ell.
    (3) CORRECTION to the scope statement in theorems/phase1/FULL_PERIODICITY_GAP.md Q4
        (repeated in LADDER.md and in the deposited note): 'no square whatsoever below
        half-length 2048, making every purely repetition-based method structurally
        inapplicable' overstates the case twice over.  (a) The method's threshold is
        g(gamma) < log2 3 < 2, not 2, so absence of squares is not decisive.  (b) The
        real obstruction is quantitative -- sup_ell r(ell) must exceed g(gamma) with
        enough room for ell*(r-g) to beat the log-height penalty -- and that is a
        statement about a limsup, not about the existence of one square.
    (4) What IS still true: no repetition-based method of THIS shape reaches Thue-Morse,
        because r(ell) does not exceed g by a margin that grows with ell.  The structural
        ceiling stands; only its justification needed correcting.
