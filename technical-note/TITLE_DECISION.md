# TITLE_DECISION.md

## Selected title

**Beyond Sturmian Words in the 3x+1 Periodicity Conjecture: A Periodic-Approximation and Arithmetic-Height Bridge**

## Rationale

- Names the conjecture precisely (3x+1 Periodicity Conjecture) without claiming to resolve it.
- "Beyond Sturmian Words" signals extension of a known result, not a from-scratch claim — matches the actual content (the repo builds on and audits an existing foundation paper's Sturmian theorem).
- "Periodic-Approximation and Arithmetic-Height Bridge" names the actual proof mechanism (Route B in this note), which is the repo's real technical contribution, rather than leading with the symbolic-class name (Rote), which is only meaningful to a reader who already knows the ladder.
- Contains no verb implying completed resolution ("proves", "solves", "settles") — the subtitle is a noun phrase describing a method, not a claim.

## Constraint checked

Avoided: "Proof of the Periodicity Conjecture" or any construction implying arbitrary aperiodic words have been settled. No candidate below uses "proof," "solves," "resolves," or "settles" in connection with the conjecture itself.

## Five alternatives, evaluated

1. **"From Sturmian to Rote: A Periodic-Approximation and Arithmetic-Height Bridge for the 3x+1 Periodicity Conjecture"**
   Clean progression framing (mirrors `LADDER.md`), restrained. Slightly weaker than the selected title because "Rote" in the title means nothing to a reader before they open the paper — the selected title's "Beyond Sturmian Words" carries the same information more accessibly.

2. **"Beyond the Sturmian Threshold: Bounded-Type Rote Sequences and the 3x+1 Periodicity Conjecture"**
   Precise about the actual headline theorem's subject (bounded-type Rote sequences). Good second choice. Slightly more technical/less inviting as an opening title; doesn't name the mechanism (periodic approximation / height) at all.

3. **"Periodic Approximation, Arithmetic Height, and Symbolic Complexity 2n: Extending the 3x+1 Periodicity Bridge Beyond Sturmian Words"**
   Most complete keyword coverage (complexity 2n, periodic approximation, arithmetic height). Rejected as primary: too long and reads as a list of keywords rather than a title; complexity threshold value is a mid-level detail, not what a reader needs in the title.

4. **"Arithmetic Height and the Limits of Periodic Approximation in the 3x+1 Periodicity Conjecture"**
   Honest, understated, foregrounds the open-problem framing (appropriate given Section 19). Rejected as primary because it undersells what **is** actually proved (the bounded-type theorem) — a title should not read as though the paper is mostly negative results.

5. **"Bounded-Type Complementary Symmetric Rote Sequences and the 3x+1 Periodicity Conjecture: A Periodic-Approximation Bridge"**
   The most technically exact title — states the precise proved subclass by name. Rejected as primary only because "complementary symmetric Rote sequences" is dense for a title's first clause; better suited as a running head or as the opening sentence of the Abstract, which the note does use verbatim.

## Decision

Keep the proposed primary title. Alternative 2 is the recommended fallback if the primary is judged too mechanism-forward for the target venue/readership; alternative 5 is the recommended fallback if precision is prioritized over accessibility.
