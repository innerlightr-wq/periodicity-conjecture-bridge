## The single question

> For every irrational `\gamma` with bounded partial quotients (`\sup_n a_n=A<\infty`), does the audited intercept-`0` CS-Rote proof produce periodic approximants with surplus tending to `+\infty`?

## Discipline

Not "does `A=\sup a_n` plug into the Phase 5 formulas" — the whole point is to **reconstruct the witness chain from BHZ's primary theorem** against genuinely non-eventually-periodic bounded examples, watching for any place periodicity was silently load-bearing. Adversarial controls, specified by the task: partial quotients coded by Thue–Morse (`\{1,2\}`), by the Fibonacci word (`\{1,2\}`), and randomized bounded sequences. No expansion of the frozen quadratic theorem; no work on `p(n)>2n`; no paper.
