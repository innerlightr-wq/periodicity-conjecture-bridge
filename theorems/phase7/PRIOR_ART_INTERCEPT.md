# PRIOR_ART_INTERCEPT.md

## What was searched

Initial critical exponent by Sturmian intercept; Ostrowski digits and repetitions; intercept-dependent recurrence/repetition functions; CS Rote sequences at arbitrary intercept; Rote critical exponents; Rote initial powers; Diophantine exponents of Sturmian/Rote words; repetition functions and irrationality criteria.

## Findings

**Initial critical exponent by intercept — already the primary source for this whole phase.** Berthé–Holton–Zamboni (2006) is itself exactly this literature; re-read directly for the general (not `\omega(-\alpha)`-specific) case in `BHZ_GENERAL_INTERCEPT_MAP.md`. No *additional* primary source found materially extending their general Corollary 3.5 formula.

**CS Rote sequences at arbitrary intercept.** No source found studying the CS-Rote transform of a *general*-intercept Sturmian word specifically (as opposed to the characteristic sequence or its two canonical shift-preimages, which is what Dvořáková–Medková–Pelantová 2020 and the Rote-literature audits in Phases 1–2 cover). This appears to be genuinely unstudied territory, not merely unfound — the CS-Rote construction itself (`u_i=v_i\oplus v_{i+1}`) is intercept-agnostic by construction (any Sturmian `u`, any intercept, gives a valid CS Rote `v`), so there is no structural reason the literature *should* single out general intercepts, but no source addressing the *repetition/irrationality* question at general intercept for Rote sequences was found.

**Diophantine exponents of Sturmian/Rote words at general intercept.** Adjacent literature (Fischler–Rivoal-type Diophantine exponent papers, found in earlier phases' searches) generally studies the *characteristic* word or its language as a whole, not intercept-indexed repetition margins. No direct hit.

## Verdict

No prior-art hit for the specific question this phase investigates (does the BHZ margin, or an equivalent, stay positive uniformly over admissible Ostrowski digit sequences). Every classical ingredient used (BHZ's own Corollary 3.5 and Proposition 2.1/Lemma 2.2 exceptional-orbit structure) is properly attributed to the primary source; the *combination* — tracking margin against Ostrowski digits specifically to certify a Rote-transferred irrationality bridge — is not found elsewhere, reported as a negative search result with the standing caveat (absence of a hit is evidence, not proof, of absence).
