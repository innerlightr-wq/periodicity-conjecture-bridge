# METHOD_BOUNDARY.md

## A. `r < \log_23` with good (even zero) discrepancy

Take `\theta=0` (perfectly balanced roots) and `r=1.5<\log_23\approx1.58496`. `REPETITION_SURPLUS_THEOREM.md`'s bracket: `r-\max(1,\gamma\log_23)`. At any density `\gamma\ge1/\log_23\approx0.631`, `\max(1,\gamma\log_23)=\gamma\log_23`, and this **exceeds** `1.5` once `\gamma>1.5/\log_23\approx0.9464`. **At density `\gamma=0.95`, margin `=1.5-0.95\times1.58496=1.5-1.5057=-0.0057<0`** — negative, **even with zero discrepancy**. This is exactly Phase 1's `BRIDGE_CONTROLS.md` Control 3 construction, now re-derived from the clean formula rather than located by search: `r<\log_23` genuinely fails at high density, regardless of how good the discrepancy is, because the `\max(1,\gamma\log_23)` term alone (with no discrepancy penalty at all) already exceeds `r`.

## B. Squares (`r=2`) with linear discrepancy

Take `r=2`, `\theta\to c>0` fixed (linear discrepancy, `D(W_n)=c\ell_n`). Margin `= 2-\max(1,\gamma\log_23)-c\log_23`. Since `\max(1,\gamma\log_23)<\log_23` always, margin `>2-\log_23-c\log_23=(2-\log_23)-c\log_23\approx0.415-1.585c`. **Margin turns negative once `c>0.415/1.585\approx0.262`.** Combined with the hard combinatorial cap `\theta\le\gamma(1-\gamma)\le0.25<0.262` (`PHASE1_FREEZE.md` §2) — **this is exactly why squares are unconditionally safe for *any actual word*: the worst achievable `\theta` (`0.25`) sits just under the failure threshold (`0.262`) with a positive margin (`\approx0.0116` in the tightest case, `\gamma\to1`).** A **hypothetical** word with `\theta>0.262` (impossible combinatorially, per the cap, but illustrative) would fall outside the method even at `r=2$ — confirming the cap, not the exponent alone, is what makes squares unconditional.

## C. Thue–Morse: the initial-power hypothesis fails entirely

No square below half-length `2048` (Phase 1's own verified fact, cited from the source paper's Appendix A). **The method is inapplicable by construction** — there is no witness sequence `(W_n)` to even attempt the argument with, `\ell_n\to\infty` and `r_n\ge2` (or `>\log_23`) cannot both hold since no such `W_n$ exists at any tested scale, and Thue–Morse is known to be square-free beyond its prefix entirely (a classical fact about the Thue–Morse morphism, `01\to0110,\ldots$, going well beyond what this audit re-derives). **This is not evidence about `\Phi(\text{Thue–Morse})`'s rationality either way** — the method simply has nothing to say, exactly the `FULL_PERIODICITY_GAP.md` Q4 structural limitation, reconfirmed here as a boundary case rather than a counterexample.

## D. `W=0^a1^a`: revisit with the exact machinery of this phase

Discrepancy: `D(0^a1^a)=a/2` exactly (elementary: max deviation at the midpoint, `k_a=0`, expected `a\cdot(a/(2a))=a/2`, deviation `a/2`). At `\ell=2a`, `\theta=D/\ell=(a/2)/(2a)=1/4$ — **exactly the combinatorial cap's own extremal value** (`\gamma(1-\gamma)=1/2\times1/2=1/4` at `\gamma=1/2`, confirming `0^a1^a` is precisely Phase 1's identified worst-case-discrepancy family, not an accident). At `r=2`: margin `=2-\max(1,0.5\times1.585)-0.25\times1.585=2-1-0.396=0.604>0` — **the bridge survives `0^a1^a` at `r=2`, confirming Phase 1's own re-derived finding** that this word (Remark 4.3's *bounded-constant-defeating* example) does **not** defeat the *sharpened* discrepancy-rate bridge. It only defeats the **cruder, unsharpened, bounded-constant** height bound the source paper's own Remark 4.3 was pointing at, a genuinely different (weaker) claim than the one this phase's theorems make.

## Is `r>\max(1,\gamma\log_23)` sharp — for this method

**Yes, sharp for this method, at the level of the leading-order asymptotic term.** At exactly `r=\max(1,\gamma\log_23)` (equality), the leading `\Theta(\ell)` term of the surplus vanishes identically, leaving only the `o(\ell)` correction terms (`-\lceil D\rceil\log_23-\log_2\ell`) — which are **negative** for any nonzero discrepancy, so the surplus `\to-\infty` unless `D\equiv0` exactly, in which case the surplus is `\Theta(\log\ell)\cdot(-1)$, still `\to-\infty$ from the `-\log_2\ell` term alone. **The threshold cannot be weakened for this specific method**, i.e. for the specific triangle-inequality bound `(†)` in `ABSTRACT_IRRATIONALITY_CRITERION.md` — a different, less lossy archimedean bound (e.g. the reduced-fraction refinement noted in `SURPLUS_THEOREM.md` §3–4) could in principle push the threshold slightly, but **not** for the bound actually used throughout this phase.

**This says nothing about sharpness for irrationality itself.** `FALSIFICATION_REPORT.md` C6 (Phase 1) already establishes the bridge is sufficient, not necessary — words below this threshold can still be irrational, certified (or not) by entirely different means (the counting route, or no known means at all). **The threshold `\log_23` is a property of this specific proof technique, not of the arithmetic truth being sought.**
